# Benchmark

mbglot on Tatoeba sentences in 72 languages (see eval/tatoeba). Generated by `moon run eval/run`; do not edit.

`franc` is `Mode::Franc` (franc-min's ranking alone), `enhanced` is `Mode::Enhanced` (the default). Accuracy counts every detection. Group accuracy also counts a language of the expected language's group as right (bos/hrv/srp, ind/zlm, hin/bho/mai/mag, run/kin). Reliable precision is the share of reliable detections that are right, and coverage the share of samples with a reliable detection.

The samples of each language and length alternate between a dev half and a test half. Weights and thresholds are chosen on the dev half; the test half is held out, and its figures come first.
